02 / COMPANY

MABCOR FACILITIES SOLUTIONS

Infrastructure capability with the assets to deliver.

Mabcor is a South African multidisciplinary contractor serving infrastructure, built-environment, industrial and mining operations.

Established in 2008 and formally incorporated in 2014, the business combines hands-on construction capability with facilities services, heavy plant and a growing logistics operation.
